About FreshCoat

Founded in 2024, FreshCoat Laundry Services has been dedicated to providing top-notch laundry solutions to households and businesses. Our mission is to make laundry day effortless so our customers can focus on what matters most.

We use state-of-the-art equipment and eco-friendly detergents to ensure your clothes come back fresh, clean, and well cared for. Every garment is handled with the utmost attention to detail.

Our team of trained professionals is committed to delivering consistent quality, timely service, and exceptional customer support. We don't just clean clothes — we build trust, one load at a time.
